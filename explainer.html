🧠 Brain Mechanics & Features

  • Compound-Eye Vision: The whole screen is split into a left and right eye and turned into brightness, darkness, motion and colour maps (8x8 up to 320x240). About 2,000 of the fly's own visual neurons each watch one patch of the screen, and they were picked because they wire straight into the fly's motor commands.
  • Target Lock & Camera Tracking: The fly follows one opponent (ignoring itself and the HUD), turns the camera toward them smoothly, and sweeps to search when nobody is in view. Turn on Shift Lock in Roblox.
  • 59,310 Neurons Read: Decisions come from everything downstream of the eyes (central brain, nerve cord, motor neurons), not only the 1,314 descending neurons.
  • Fighting Instincts & Real Controls: Close in when far, strike when in reach, block or dodge incoming attacks. Movement and block keys are held like a player holds them.
  • Arena & Calibration: Warm the fly up in a simulated JJS fight ([A]), and show it where your health bars are once ([C]).
  • 3D Brain: All 166,700 neurons in 3D in your browser, glowing when they fire more than usual. Show the fly a looming shadow and watch its escape circuit light up ([B]).
  • Casino: The fly gambles its life at Higher or Lower against a bot ([G]). It sees the cards with its eyes, learns from dopamine, and its real fear circuit drives a terror meter; scared, it bets small.
  • Pattern Recognition: Evaluates recent movement sequences and threat changes to predict enemy bursts and dashes.
  • Dopamine Reinforcement: Dopamine is surprise: a hit the fly didn't expect releases it on its reward neurons, damage it didn't see coming hits its punishment neurons. The fly tries moves on purpose, keeps the ones that pay off and drops the ones that don't.
  • Watch & Learn: Train Mode teaches the exact same brain wiring that Play Mode uses, so imitation and reinforcement learning build on each other.
